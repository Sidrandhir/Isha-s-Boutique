The Founder

Archana Patil

Archana Patil started Isha's Boutique from home in 2003, driven purely by passion. Coming from a humble, middle-class background, she taught herself the basics, connecting with clients, understanding their needs, and exploring her own creativity, before formalising it with a Diploma in Fashion Designing & Garment Making from INIFD, Jalgaon, on top of a BA in Psychology and an MA in Public Administration, both from Deogiri College.

In 2018 she was named an INIFD Achievers Award winner. Today she isn't just a fashion designer but the owner of a boutique where custom tailoring is done at its finest, sarees and navari draping, bridal blouses and designer gowns, kids' festive wear, and the hands-on training that passes the craft on to the next generation.

What Guides Us

Four Rules We Don't Break

01

Craft First

Every seam is finished the way it would be for our own family. The inside of the garment is as neat as the outside, no shortcuts on fit or finishing.

→
02

Tradition Meets Trend

Navari draping, paithani borders, block prints, kantha, zari work, regional techniques from across India, updated for how families actually dress today.

→
03

Personal Attention

Every client is fitted and consulted directly. This is a boutique, not an assembly line, and the person who takes your measurements is the person who stitches the piece.

→
04

Mentorship

Sharing the craft through the Diploma in Fashion Designing and hands-on coaching, so the skill outlives any one order and stays in Jalgaon.

Our Journey

Twenty-Three Years,
One Stitch at a Time

2003

Stitching From Home

Isha's Boutique began the way most trusted tailoring houses do, working from home, driven by passion, with careful fittings and a growing circle of happy customers in Jalgaon.

Growing Years

Becoming an Occasion Address

As wedding, birthday and festival orders grew, the boutique became known for sarees, bridal blouses and celebration wear, handling whole family wardrobes, not just single garments.

2018

INIFD Achievers Award

Recognised by the International Institute of Fashion Design, Jalgaon, for the standard of work coming out of the boutique.

2024

MEDP Certification

Completed a More Entrepreneurship Development Programme, sharpening the business side of running the boutique and its training arm.

Today

All-India Styles, One Address

Today Isha's Boutique brings stitching traditions from across India under one roof, kids to bridal, still cut, fitted and finished by hand in Jalgaon.

Stitch School

Learn It Properly.

The Diploma in Fashion Designing & Garment Making Course covers the fundamentals; the advanced course masters blouse and dress construction. Both come with practice time and a placement facility to build real confidence, not just a certificate.

Beginners, hobbyists and serious apprentices all learn at the same table, to the same standard of finishing Archana holds herself to.
