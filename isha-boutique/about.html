The Founder

Archana Patil

Archana Patil founded Isha's Boutique in Jalgaon after building a reputation, garment by garment, for stitching that fits the way it's meant to and finishes the way a customer imagines it. Over 15+ years that reputation grew from a neighbourhood tailor into a name families across the city rely on for celebrations, festivals and everyday wear alike.

Her work runs across every kind of stitching a household needs — children's party and festive wear, blouses and lehengas, sarees draped in styles from across India, salwar suits, and the small alterations that make a ready-made piece feel custom. That same hands-on knowledge is now passed on through master classes, internships and one-on-one coaching.

What Guides Us

Four Rules We Don't Break

01

Craft First

Every seam is finished the way it would be for our own family. The inside of the garment is as neat as the outside — no shortcuts on fit or finishing.

→
02

Tradition Meets Trend

Regional techniques from across India — paithani borders, block prints, kantha, zari work — updated for how families actually dress today.

→
03

Personal Attention

Every client is fitted and consulted directly. This is a boutique, not an assembly line — and the person who takes your measurements is the person who stitches the piece.

→
04

Mentorship

Sharing the craft through classes and internships, so the skill outlives any one order and stays in Jalgaon.

Our Journey

Fifteen Years,
One Stitch at a Time

The Beginning

Stitching for Family & Neighbours

Isha's Boutique began the way most trusted tailoring houses do — with word of mouth, careful fittings, and a growing circle of happy customers in Jalgaon.

Growing Reputation

Becoming an Occasion Address

As wedding, birthday and festival orders grew, the boutique became known for celebration wear — handling whole family wardrobes, not just single garments.

Sharing the Craft

Master Classes & Internships

With demand from aspiring tailors and designers, the boutique opened its doors to students — through structured master classes, hands-on internships and personal coaching.

Today

All-India Styles, One Address

Today Isha's Boutique brings stitching traditions from across India under one roof — still cut, fitted and finished by hand in Jalgaon.

Stitch School

Learn It Properly.

Beginners, hobbyists and serious apprentices all learn at the same table. Small batches, real garments, and the same standard of finishing we hold ourselves to.

Master classes cover fundamentals, draping and pattern work. Internships are on-the-job, at the boutique, on live orders.
