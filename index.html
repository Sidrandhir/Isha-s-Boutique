The Atelier

One Atelier.
One Pair of Eyes.
Since 2003.

Isha's Boutique began at home in 2003, driven by passion rather than a plan. Archana Patil went on to formalise that instinct with a Diploma in Fashion Designing from INIFD Jalgaon, on top of a BA in Psychology and an MA in Public Administration, and in 2018 was named an INIFD Achievers Award winner for it.

Nothing here is mass produced. Archana still cuts and checks every order herself, whether it's a navari saree, a bridal blouse or a toddler's first festive frock.
